Field Notes & Diversions:

01 • Optics Maintenance

The 3 Golden Rules for Cleaning Camera Glass on the Go

Never exhale hot moisture onto cold lens coatings—it leaves chemical minerals behind. Always brush away loose grit first, then use a dry antimicrobial microfiber weave in circular sweeps from the center outward.

02 • Travel Essentials

The Minimalist Carry: Ditch Single-Use Chemical Wipes

Ditch bulky spray bottles and alcohol wipes at TSA security. A dedicated, washable microfiber cloth keeps your laptop, eyewear and phone crystal-clear without stripping oleophobic (oil-repelling coating) factory screens.
